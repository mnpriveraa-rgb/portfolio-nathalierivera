About Me

My career started in banking compliance — seven years at HSBC Philippines, three at Capital One before that — auditing customer interactions and holding the line on accuracy.

Since moving into virtual assistance and executive support, I've applied that same discipline to building things that didn't exist yet: a 28-document SOP library for a landscape lighting company, a 500-contact CRM pipeline for a real estate investor, referral programs, sales collateral, content calendars.

Years of managing calendars, inboxes, CRMs, and client communications across multiple industries have taught me how to walk into a business, find what's disorganized or slipping through the cracks, and fix it without needing to be micromanaged.

My goal isn't to be a task-taker. It's to become the person a business owner stops worrying about.
